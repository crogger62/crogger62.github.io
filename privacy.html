Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

What the app accesses

YTMusicPython is a personal, self-hosted script. With your permission, it uses Google OAuth to access your YouTube account (YouTube Data API scope) in order to read and manage your own playlists and library, for example listing, creating, and updating playlists.

How data is used and stored

Data from your Google account is used only to carry out the playlist actions you run. OAuth tokens and any data retrieved are stored only on the local machine where the script runs. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party, and do not use it for advertising or to train AI or machine learning models.

YTMusicPython's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/crogger62.
